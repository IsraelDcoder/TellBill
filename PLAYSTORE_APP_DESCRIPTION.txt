📱 **TELLBILL: Intelligent Invoicing for Contractors**

Protect your revenue. Avoid disputes. Get paid faster.

TellBill is the all-in-one invoicing and revenue protection platform built specifically for contractors, freelancers, and small business owners who demand results. Turn voice notes into professional invoices in seconds, capture scope proof to eliminate disputes, and get paid on time — every time.

✨ **WHAT YOU CAN DO**

🎤 **Voice-to-Invoice**: Record work details, capture client info, and instantly generate professional invoices. No more manual data entry. No more paperwork.

📸 **Scope Proof**: Capture photos and timestamps of your work. Request client sign-off. Have dispute-ready documentation at your fingertips.

🧾 **Receipt Scanning**: Extract data from receipts automatically. Track expenses. Organize your finances without the headache.

💰 **Smart Invoicing**: Create unlimited invoices with custom tax settings, payment terms, and branding. Send via email, SMS, or WhatsApp — your clients prefer convenience.

🔔 **Payment Tracking**: Monitor invoice status in real-time. Set payment reminders. Track overdue payments instantly.

📊 **Financial Insights**: Get a complete view of your revenue. Track which clients pay on time. Identify payment patterns.

🤝 **Client Approvals**: Share work photos and scope details with clients before invoicing. Get approval. Eliminate scope creep. Protect your margins.

🔐 **Secure & Compliant**: Your data is encrypted. Backups are automatic. Your financial information is protected.
